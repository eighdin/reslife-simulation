Money Moves

Session default

A guided financial choices simulation

Over twenty rounds, you'll face real financial decisions — the kind that show up in your first apartment, first job, and first credit card. Every choice moves two numbers: your credit score and your net worth. Nobody "loses" — you can absolutely end up in debt, and that's the point: you'll feel what it costs, without it costing you anything real. Some outcomes are certain; some are a genuine gamble, the way real financial decisions often are.

  • Twenty rounds, a couple of choices each — read the terms, pick what you'd actually do.
  • Debt you carry keeps accruing interest between rounds, and some choices carry real risk, just like in real life.
  • Your live score feeds the shared leaderboard for this session.
